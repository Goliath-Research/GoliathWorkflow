\section{Goal}

\texttt{MethylDetector} identifies \emph{differentially methylated positions}
(DMPs) between two methylation centroids (e.g.\ healthy vs.\ disease) with:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Statistical rigour} --- Histogram-derived Mann--Whitney
        testing with FDR correction.
  \item \textbf{Biological relevance} --- A single canonical score
        (\texttt{effect\_size}) that penalises positions where the
        distributions overlap heavily or where within-group variance is large.
  \item \textbf{Computational tractability} --- A staged funnel that avoids
        expensive non-parametric testing and ECDF work on millions of
        positions that cannot pass the biological filter.
\end{itemize}

All distribution comparisons use the \textbf{ECDF only}; no Beta, Normal, or
Beta-Binomial models are used.

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Staged Pipeline}

\begin{description}
  \item[Stage 1 --- Pre-filter (cheap).]
        Start from positions present in both centroids after the coverage
        filter. Compute \(|\Delta\mu| = |\hat\mu_1 - \hat\mu_2|\) from centroid
        means \(\hat\mu = S_x/N\). Keep only positions with
        \(|\Delta\mu| \geq \texttt{delta\_mean\_reduction}\).

  \item[Stage 2 --- Centroid alignment handoff.]
        Carry the shared genomic coordinates forward as per-centroid indices
        so every surviving position refers to the same locus in both
        centroids.

  \item[Stage 3 --- Statistical significance test.]
        For each aligned position, compute a p-value with Mann--Whitney from
        bin counts (Section~\ref{sec:mannwhitney}).

  \item[Stage 4 --- FDR correction.]
        Two-stage Benjamini--Hochberg (\texttt{statsmodels fdr\_tsbh}) on the
        pre-filtered position set.

  \item[Stage 5 --- Statistical + heterogeneity filter.]
        Retain positions with \(q \leq \alpha\).  Optionally drop positions
        where both groups exceed \texttt{max\_tau2\_for\_dmp}
        (Section~\ref{sec:tau2}).

  \item[Stage 6 --- Lazy ECDFView construction.]
        Build \texttt{PchipInterpolator} objects only for the surviving DMP
        positions, using correct per-centroid indices.

  \item[Stage 7 --- Continuous ECDF overlap and \texttt{effect\_size}.]
        Compute overlap (Section~\ref{sec:overlap}) and the canonical
        biological score (Section~\ref{sec:effectsize}).

  \item[Stage 8 --- Biological filter.]
        Within each context, keep the minimum prefix whose cumulative
        \texttt{effect\_size} reaches \texttt{effect\_size\_coverage} of the
        total effect mass.

  \item[Stage 9 --- Optional biological-only rescue.]
        Select non-significant but high-effect positions separately, flagged
        explicitly.

  \item[Stage 10 --- Held-out top-$k$ selection.]
        Repeated stratified validation splits, cached ECDF log-likelihoods,
        and balanced accuracy choose the final top-$k$ prefix.
\end{description}

\section{Statistical Test}

\subsection{Mann--Whitney $U$ from Histogram Bin Counts}
\label{sec:mannwhitney}

The detector reconstructs the $U$ statistic directly from the centroid
\texttt{bin\_counts} without raw sample values:

\begin{equation}
  U = \sum_{b=1}^{B} \mathrm{bc1}_b \cdot \mathrm{CS2}_{b-1}
      + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{b=1}^{B} \mathrm{bc1}_b \cdot \mathrm{bc2}_b,
\end{equation}

where \(\mathrm{bc1}_b\) and \(\mathrm{bc2}_b\) are the bin counts and
\(\mathrm{CS2}_{b-1} = \sum_{k<b}\mathrm{bc2}_k\) is the cumulative count of
group~2 in all bins strictly below bin~$b$.  The first term counts
concordant pairs, the second assigns half-credit to tied pairs.

Under the null hypothesis, $U$ is approximately Normal with

\begin{equation}
  \mathbb{E}[U] = \frac{N_1 N_2}{2}, \qquad
  \mathrm{Var}(U) = \frac{N_1 N_2}{12}
    \left[(N_1+N_2+1) - \frac{\sum_b t_b(t_b^2-1)}{(N_1+N_2)(N_1+N_2-1)}\right],
\end{equation}

where \(t_b = \mathrm{bc1}_b + \mathrm{bc2}_b\) is the total count in bin~$b$
(tie correction).  The z-statistic and two-sided p-value follow:

\begin{equation}
  z = \frac{U - \mathbb{E}[U]}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(U)}},
  \qquad p = 2\,\Phi(-|z|),
\end{equation}

where $\Phi$ is the standard Normal CDF.

\subsection{Sample Variance for the Reliability Term}

Sample variances are computed from stored sufficient statistics:

\begin{equation}
  \hat\sigma^2 = \frac{S_{x^2} - S_x^2/N}{\max(N-1,\,1)}.
  \label{eq:samplevar}
\end{equation}

These variances are used in the biological reliability term and heterogeneity
filters, not in the Mann--Whitney significance gate itself.

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Between-Sample Heterogeneity (\texorpdfstring{$\tau^2$}{tau2})}
\label{sec:tau2}

A DerSimonian--Laird-style decomposition uses the stored sufficient statistics
$(\Sigma m,\,\Sigma u,\,\Sigma w x^2,\,\Sigma c^2,\,N)$ to estimate
between-sample variance without reading individual sample files.

Let \(C = \Sigma m + \Sigma u\) (total coverage sum) and
\(\theta = \Sigma m / C\) (coverage-weighted pooled mean).

The within-group heterogeneity statistic is

\begin{equation}
  Q = \Sigma w x^2 - \frac{(\Sigma m)^2}{C},
\end{equation}

and the between-sample variance estimate is

\begin{equation}
  \hat\tau^2 = \max\!\left(0,\;\frac{Q - (N-1)}{C - \Sigma c^2/C}\right).
\end{equation}

Positions where both groups exceed \texttt{max\_tau2\_for\_dmp} are dropped
before the biological filter, because large $\hat\tau^2$ indicates
heterogeneous sub-populations rather than a clean methylation shift.

\section{ECDF Overlap}
\label{sec:overlap}

The continuous ECDF overlap between two class distributions is defined as

\begin{equation}
  O = \int_0^1 \min\!\bigl(f_1(x),\,f_2(x)\bigr)\,dx,
  \label{eq:overlap}
\end{equation}

where \(f_1(x)\) and \(f_2(x)\) are PCHIP-derived PDFs obtained by
differentiating the spline CDF built from centroid \texttt{bin\_counts}.

The integral is evaluated with the trapezoidal rule on a dense grid
(default 512 points).  PDFs are renormalised before integration to guard
against small numerical drift in spline derivatives:

\begin{equation}
  \tilde{f}_k(x) = \frac{f_k(x)}
    {\displaystyle\int_0^1 f_k(t)\,dt}, \qquad k \in \{1,2\}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Discrete Bhattacharyya Approximation}

In the fast path the overlap is approximated by the discrete Bhattacharyya
coefficient computed directly from bin counts:

\begin{equation}
  O_\mathrm{Bh} = \sum_{b=1}^{B} \sqrt{p_{1b}\cdot p_{2b}},
  \qquad p_{kb} = \frac{\mathrm{bc}_{kb}}{\sum_{b'}\mathrm{bc}_{kb'}}.
\end{equation}

This is \(\mathcal{O}(n_\mathrm{pos} \cdot B)\) and is used for the full
position set before the statistical test reduces it.

\section{Canonical Effect Size}
\label{sec:effectsize}

The biological importance score is

\begin{equation}
  \boxed{
    \mathrm{effect\_size}
    = |\Delta\mu|
      \cdot (1 - O)
      \cdot \exp\!\bigl(-\lambda_\mathrm{var}
            (\sqrt{\hat\sigma_1^2} + \sqrt{\hat\sigma_2^2})\bigr),
  }
  \label{eq:effectsize}
\end{equation}

where:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \(|\Delta\mu| = |\hat\mu_1 - \hat\mu_2|\) --- absolute mean
        methylation difference.
  \item \(O\) --- continuous ECDF overlap from
        Equation~\eqref{eq:overlap}; \(1 - O\) is the distributional
        separation (\(0\) = complete overlap, \(1\) = no overlap).
  \item \(\exp(-\lambda_\mathrm{var}(\sqrt{\hat\sigma_1^2}+\sqrt{\hat\sigma_2^2}))\)
        --- reliability penalty that reduces the score for diffuse,
        heterogeneous positions.
  \item \(\lambda_\mathrm{var}\) (default \(2.0\)) --- variance penalty
        strength.
\end{itemize}

Both variances are treated independently; no equal-variance assumption is
made. The variances are the estimator from Equation~\eqref{eq:samplevar} and
act only through the biological reliability term, so a diffuse locus is
down-weighted even though the significance gate itself remains non-parametric.

The score is clipped to \([0,1]\) after computation.

\subsection{Effect Size Coverage Biological Filter}

Within each methylation context, positions are sorted by \texttt{effect\_size}
descending.  The minimum prefix whose cumulative mass reaches the target fraction
is retained:

\begin{equation}
  k^* = \min\left\{k\;:\;\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} e_i}{\sum_{i} e_i}
                   \geq \texttt{effect\_size\_coverage}\right\},
\end{equation}

where \(e_i\) is the effect size of the \(i\)-th position after sorting.

\section{ECDFClassifier}
\label{sec:classifier}

MethylDetector trains an \textbf{ECDFClassifier} on the selected biological DMPs.

\subsection{Log-Likelihood}

For a new sample with methylation fractions \(x = (x_1,\ldots,x_K)\) at the
selected DMP positions, the log-likelihood for class \(k\) is

\begin{equation}
  \log L(\text{class}_k \mid x)
  = \sum_{i=1}^{K} w_i \cdot \log F'_{k,i}(x_i),
  \label{eq:loglik}
\end{equation}

where:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \(F'_{k,i}(x)\) is the PCHIP-derived PDF from the centroid of
        class \(k\) at DMP position \(i\).
  \item \(w_i = \mathrm{effect\_size}_i \;/\; \max_j(\mathrm{effect\_size}_j)\)
        --- positions with larger effect contribute more.
  \item A small floor is applied: \(F'_{k,i}(x) \geq 10^{-300}\) to
        avoid \(\log(0)\).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Posterior and Prediction}

The posterior probability of class \(k\) is obtained by
temperature-scaled softmax:

\begin{equation}
  P(\text{class}_k \mid x)
  \propto \exp\!\left(\frac{\log L(\text{class}_k \mid x)}{T}\right),
  \label{eq:posterior}
\end{equation}

where \(T \geq 0.1\) is the temperature parameter (default \(T = 2.0\)).
Prediction is \(\hat{k} = \arg\max_k P(\text{class}_k \mid x)\).

The ECDFClassifier stores the \texttt{bin\_counts} histograms per DMP
position and uses the same PCHIP PDF
model as the DMP detection stage, keeping detection and classification
fully consistent.

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{FDR Correction}

After computing per-position p-values, multiple-testing correction uses the
two-stage Benjamini--Hochberg procedure (\texttt{statsmodels fdr\_tsbh}).

\textbf{Note on liberality.}  FDR is applied to the pre-filtered position
subset (after \texttt{delta\_mean\_reduction}).  Q-values are therefore
liberal relative to testing all positions.  For prostate-cancer-scale data
(CG context: \(\approx\!2000\) significant positions out of \(4.3\) million),
the practical effect is small because the pre-filter removes positions that
would not survive the biological filter regardless of significance.
