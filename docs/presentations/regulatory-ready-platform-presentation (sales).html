Claim boundary

What the platform is — and isn't

Scope honesty, stated upfront: this is how you sell process control to a buyer who audits every clinical claim.

Boundary statement
  • Platform enforces process and configuration controls — it does not choose clinical design.
  • Platform assembles evidence packages — it does not certify them with any authority.
  • Architecture, SaMD profiles, and filled feasibility packages are not FDA clearance or approval.
  • Novel diagnostics may need De Novo or PMA — not only 510(k); labs may also implicate CLIA.

Three pillars

What holds the platform up

R&D agility, production-scale compute efficiency, and SaMD-oriented process controls — in that order of dependency.

01 R&D agility

Without coding chaos

Real production-scale

Against statistical contamination

Two editions

Community vs. Enterprise / SaMD

Community Edition

For academic traction

Open source under AGPL-3.0 (copyleft for network / SaaS use)
  • Engine — local workflow engine
  • Process pack — standard DNA methylation pack
  • Tools — basic CLI tools
  • Profiles — research profiles (e.g. samd_research)
Enterprise / SaMD Edition

For closing the regulatory gap

Annual subscription (per worker node or deployment site) + support
  • Regulatory suite — validation evidence index + traceability
  • Pivotal SaMD ladder — samd_holdout_enrichment, samd_pivotal
  • Control plane — multi-tenant (EpiPortal UI)
  • Enterprise security — DB credentials to TLS task payloads; never plain files under /work
  • GPU support — supported NVIDIA Parabricks SamplePrep paths
